# Campaign brief: Harlow & Pine "Winter Sale"

**Range:** Harlow & Pine, Larkfield Market's own clothing range
**Issued by:** Larkfield Market head office, Clothing Visual Merchandising
**Stores:** all Larkfield Market stores with a clothing section
**Campaign period:** 26 December 2027 to 15 January 2028
**Lookbook:** [`lookbook-winter-sale.jpg`](../images/reference/lookbook-winter-sale.jpg)
**Read with:** *Harlow & Pine visual merchandising standards*, v4.2

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Objective

Replaces "The Autumn Edit" overnight on 25 December. Clear winter outerwear
stock: the mannequin moves to the puffer jacket, the best-selling sale line;
the jumper table stays the same.

## The look

Match the lookbook. In words:

- **Mannequin:** navy quilted puffer jacket (zipped), grey knitted beanie hat,
  black slim jeans, black leather ankle boots. No scarf, no overcoat.
- **Table:** unchanged from "The Autumn Edit": three neat stacks of folded
  knitted jumpers, from left to right: cream, navy, forest green.
- **Signage:** one red **"WINTER SALE 30% OFF"** sign, on the table or on a
  stand beside or behind it. The "THE AUTUMN EDIT" sign must be removed.

## Store terms

- The display must be changed overnight on 25 December, ready for opening on 26 December.
- Sale prices are applied at the till. *(Not part of the photo audit.)*
