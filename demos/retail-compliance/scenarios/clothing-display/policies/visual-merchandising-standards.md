# Harlow & Pine visual merchandising standards: front-of-section displays

**Document owner:** Larkfield Market head office, Clothing Visual Merchandising
**Applies to:** the Harlow & Pine front-of-section display (one mannequin and one
display table) in every Larkfield Market store with a clothing section
**Version:** 4.2, issued 20 August 2027

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Background

Harlow & Pine is Larkfield Market's own clothing range. The front-of-section
display is the first thing shoppers see when they enter the clothing area. Each
campaign is defined by a **lookbook**: a reference image, sent by head office,
showing how the mannequin and table should look. Store teams dress the display
to match the lookbook; field reps photograph it to confirm.

## 1. Matching the lookbook

1.1 The mannequin must wear exactly the outfit shown in the active campaign's
lookbook: every garment and accessory, and nothing else.

1.2 The display table must show the products, colours and left-to-right
arrangement shown in the lookbook.

1.3 Only the sign shown in the active lookbook may be on display, with the same
wording and colour. It may stand on the table or on a stand beside or behind it.
Signs from earlier campaigns must be removed on the day the new campaign starts.

1.4 Store fixtures vary, so the exact positions of the mannequin, table and sign
may differ from the lookbook. The outfit, the table arrangement and the sign
itself must not.

## 2. Presentation

2.1 Folded stacks must be neat: garments folded to the same size, edges aligned,
one colour per stack. Stacks must be refolded throughout the day.

2.2 No garments may be left unfolded, crumpled, hanging off the table or on the floor.

2.3 The mannequin must be fully dressed, upright and on its base.

2.4 Price tickets must be attached to every garment on the table. *(Checked by
hand: tickets are too small to verify from photographs.)*

## 3. Sizes

3.1 Each stack must hold sizes S, M, L and XL, smallest at the top. *(Checked by
hand.)*

## 4. Audit evidence

4.1 On each visit, the field rep photographs:

- **Display (required):** the mannequin and table together, from where a shopper
  would first see them.
- **Close-ups (optional):** for example, the table alone, to show stack tidiness.

4.2 A requirement that has no usable photograph is recorded as "cannot assess",
never as compliant.
