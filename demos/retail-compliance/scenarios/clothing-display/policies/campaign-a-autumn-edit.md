# Campaign brief: Harlow & Pine "The Autumn Edit"

**Range:** Harlow & Pine, Larkfield Market's own clothing range
**Issued by:** Larkfield Market head office, Clothing Visual Merchandising
**Stores:** all Larkfield Market stores with a clothing section
**Campaign period:** 1 September to 25 December 2027
**Lookbook:** [`lookbook-autumn-edit.jpg`](../images/reference/lookbook-autumn-edit.jpg)
**Read with:** *Harlow & Pine visual merchandising standards*, v4.2

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Objective

Launch the autumn and winter range with a "complete the look" display: shoppers
should see the full outfit and buy more than one piece.

## The look

Match the lookbook. In words:

- **Mannequin:** camel wool overcoat (worn open), cream roll-neck jumper,
  burgundy knitted scarf around the neck, dark indigo straight jeans, white
  leather trainers.
- **Table:** three neat stacks of folded knitted jumpers, from left to right:
  cream, navy, forest green.
- **Signage:** one black **"THE AUTUMN EDIT"** sign, on the table or on a
  stand beside or behind it.

## Store terms

- The display must be dressed by store opening on 1 September.
- Stores should refold the table at least three times a day.
- Sales are reported weekly.
