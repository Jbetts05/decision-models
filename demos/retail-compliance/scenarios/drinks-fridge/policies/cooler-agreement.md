# Fizzwell branded cooler agreement: Cornerstop Convenience

**Document owner:** Fizzwell Drinks Ltd., Field Sales
**Retailer:** Cornerstop Convenience (all stores with a Fizzwell cooler)
**Applies to:** every Fizzwell-supplied cooler, in every campaign
**Version:** 2.0, issued 3 February 2027

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Background

Fizzwell supplies, installs and maintains branded coolers free of charge. In
return, the store agrees that the cooler is used only for Fizzwell products and
merchandised to the active campaign planogram. Branded coolers are an expensive
investment, and their return depends on what shoppers see through the door.

## 1. Cooler purity

1.1 Only Fizzwell products may be stocked in a Fizzwell cooler. Any other
brand's product, including water, milk, sandwiches or store-brand drinks, is a
breach of this agreement, wherever it sits in the cooler.

## 2. Availability

2.1 Every shelf must be fully stocked across its whole width. A gap is any
place where the front row is missing, so that the shelf or the back of the
cooler is visible.

2.2 Products must face forward, with the brand name readable through the door.

2.3 Stock must be rotated by date. *(Checked by hand, not from photographs.)*

## 3. Cooler condition

3.1 The interior lights and the illuminated "FIZZWELL" header must be switched
on whenever the store is open.

3.2 The cooler must hold its products below 5 °C. *(Read from the cooler's
thermometer by hand, not from photographs.)*

3.3 The door glass must be clean and free of anything other than the current
campaign's door sticker.

## 4. Pricing

4.1 Every shelf must have a price label on its front edge, readable through the door.

4.2 Price labels must show the prices agreed with Cornerstop Convenience for the
active campaign. Cornerstop sets its own selling prices; Fizzwell funds the
agreed promotional prices.

## 5. Campaign material

5.1 Only the active campaign's door sticker and shelf labels may be displayed.
Material from earlier campaigns must be removed on the day a campaign ends.

## 6. Audit evidence

6.1 On each visit, the field rep photographs:

- **Full cooler (required):** the whole cooler, straight on, door closed, with
  the header, every shelf and the door sticker in view.
- **Top-shelf close-up (optional):** the top shelf, its price label and the door
  sticker, close enough to read them.

6.2 A requirement that has no usable photograph is recorded as "cannot assess",
never as compliant.
