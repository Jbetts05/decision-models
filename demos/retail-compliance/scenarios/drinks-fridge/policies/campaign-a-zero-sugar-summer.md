# Campaign brief: Fizzwell "Zero Sugar Summer"

**Brand:** Fizzwell Drinks Ltd.
**Retailer:** Cornerstop Convenience, all stores with a Fizzwell cooler
**Campaign period:** 7 June to 4 July 2027
**Read with:** *Fizzwell branded cooler agreement*, v2.0

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Objective

Grow Fizzwell Zero's share of cold drinks during the summer by giving it the
top shelf and a price incentive. Target: +25% Zero cans sold.

## Cooler planogram

Price labels show the prices agreed with Cornerstop Convenience; Fizzwell funds
the "2 for £2" promotion.

| Shelf | Product | Agreed price label |
|---|---|---|
| Top | Fizzwell Zero cans (black) | **"2 for £2"** yellow promotional label |
| Second | Fizzwell Cola cans (red) | £1.20 white label |
| Third | Fizzwell Orange cans (orange) | £1.20 white label |
| Bottom | Fizzwell Cola 500 ml bottles | £1.80 white label |

## Door sticker

One round black-and-yellow **"ZERO SUGAR SUMMER"** sticker on the door glass.

## Store terms

- Stores receive a £20 merchandising payment for each compliant audit.
- Stores should restock the cooler before the lunchtime peak every day.
- Head office reviews sell-out data weekly.
