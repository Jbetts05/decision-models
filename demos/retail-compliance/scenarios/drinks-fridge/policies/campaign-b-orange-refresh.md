# Campaign brief: Fizzwell "Orange Refresh"

**Brand:** Fizzwell Drinks Ltd.
**Retailer:** Cornerstop Convenience, all stores with a Fizzwell cooler
**Campaign period:** 5 July to 1 August 2027
**Read with:** *Fizzwell branded cooler agreement*, v2.0

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Objective

Follow "Zero Sugar Summer" by moving Fizzwell Orange to the top shelf with a
multibuy, ahead of the school holidays. Fizzwell Zero moves to the third shelf.
Fizzwell Orange is a no-added-sugar recipe outside the HFSS promotion
restrictions, so the multibuy is permitted.

## Cooler planogram

Price labels show the prices agreed with Cornerstop Convenience; Fizzwell funds
the "3 for £3" promotion.

| Shelf | Product | Agreed price label |
|---|---|---|
| Top | Fizzwell Orange cans (orange) | **"3 for £3"** yellow promotional label |
| Second | Fizzwell Cola cans (red) | £1.20 white label |
| Third | Fizzwell Zero cans (black) | £1.20 white label |
| Bottom | Fizzwell Cola 500 ml bottles | £1.80 white label |

No "2 for £2" labels may remain anywhere in the cooler.

## Door sticker

Replace the "ZERO SUGAR SUMMER" sticker with one round orange-and-white
**"ORANGE REFRESH"** sticker in the same position. The old sticker must be
removed, not covered.

## Store terms

- Stores receive a £20 merchandising payment for each compliant audit.
- The new planogram must be in place by store opening on 5 July.
