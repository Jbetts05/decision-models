# Larkfield Market: Brightmoor Coffee execution standards

**Document owner:** Brightmoor Coffee Co., Field Sales
**Retailer:** Larkfield Market (all store formats)
**Applies to:** every Brightmoor campaign, unless a campaign brief says otherwise
**Version:** 3.1, issued 12 January 2027

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Why this matters

Our trade investment only works when the shelf is built the way we agreed with
the retailer. Industry audits repeatedly find that a large share of planned
displays are not executed as specified. These standards define the baseline
every Brightmoor fixture must meet, in every store, on every visit.

## 1. Availability

1.1 Every Brightmoor SKU listed in the active campaign brief must be on the shelf.

1.2 There must be no empty gaps within the Brightmoor block. A gap is any space
of one product width or more where the shelf back or shelf base is visible.

1.3 Stock should be rotated so the shortest date is at the front. *(Field reps
confirm this by checking pack dates by hand.)*

## 2. Facing and presentation

2.1 Packs must be pulled forward to the front edge of the shelf ("faced up").

2.2 Every pack must stand upright, with its front label facing the shopper. No
packs on their side, upside down, or turned so the side panel faces out.

2.3 No crushed, torn, opened or visibly damaged packs may remain on display.

2.4 The shelf must be free of litter, spillage and loose packaging.

## 3. Brand blocking

3.1 All Brightmoor packs must sit together in one continuous block on the
designated shelf.

3.2 No other brand's products may be placed inside or between Brightmoor
packs. Competitor products directly beside the block are acceptable.

## 4. Pricing

4.1 Each Brightmoor SKU must have a shelf-edge label (SEL) on the shelf edge
directly below it.

4.2 Shelf-edge labels must be fully visible: not covered by packs, point-of-sale
material or other labels.

4.3 The price on each shelf-edge label must match the promotional or standard
price agreed with Larkfield Market for the active campaign. Larkfield Market
sets its own selling prices; Brightmoor funds the agreed promotional prices.
Promotional prices use yellow promotional labels; standard prices use white labels.

4.4 Prices must also be correct on the till. *(Confirmed by a test purchase,
not from shelf photographs.)*

## 5. Point-of-sale material (POSM)

5.1 Only point-of-sale material listed in the active campaign brief may be
displayed. Material from expired campaigns must be removed on the day the
campaign ends.

5.2 No more than two wobblers (shelf talkers) per shelf level.

5.3 Point-of-sale material must not hide a Brightmoor product's name or a
shelf-edge price.

## 6. Audit evidence

6.1 On each visit, the field rep uploads:

- **Full-bay photograph (required):** one straight-on photograph showing every
  shelf and the shelf edges, in normal store lighting. Used for placement,
  order, facings, gaps, brand blocking and competitor products.
- **Shelf-edge close-up (required for each shelf with campaign activity):** the
  shelf-edge labels and any point-of-sale material on that shelf, close enough
  for prices to be read. Prices are not judged from the full-bay photograph.
- **Detail photographs (optional):** for example, a close-up of a damaged pack
  or a missing header card, when they support a finding.

The number of photographs therefore varies from visit to visit.

6.2 If a photograph is blurred, obstructed (for example, by a shopper or
trolley) or does not show what it is meant to show, it must be retaken. A
requirement that has no usable photograph is recorded as "cannot assess",
never as compliant.

6.3 The store manager signs the visit report before the rep leaves.
