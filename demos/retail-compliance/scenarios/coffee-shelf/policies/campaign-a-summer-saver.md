# Campaign brief: Brightmoor "Summer Saver"

**Brand:** Brightmoor Coffee Co.
**Retailer:** Larkfield Market, all stores with a hot-beverages aisle
**Campaign period:** 1 June to 28 June 2027
**Display set-up deadline:** 31 May 2027, before store opening
**Read with:** *Larkfield Market: Brightmoor Coffee execution standards*, v3.1

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Objective

Drive trial of Brightmoor Original Roast during the summer trading period with
a price-led promotion, while maintaining full range visibility. Target: +18%
Original Roast unit sales against the same period last year.

## Range and placement

The Brightmoor range must be placed on the **eye-level (middle) shelf** of the
coffee bay for the whole campaign. Shelf-edge prices are those agreed with
Larkfield Market for this promotion, which Brightmoor funds.

| SKU | Pack colour | Minimum facings | Agreed shelf-edge label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original Roast | Blue | 3 | **£3.50** yellow promotional label |
| Dark Roast | Black | 2 | £4.00 white label |
| Decaf | Green | 2 | £4.00 white label |

Arrange the range from left to right in this order: **Original Roast, Dark
Roast, Decaf.**

Brightmoor Hazelnut is not part of this campaign and must not be displayed.

## Point-of-sale material

- **One** "Summer Saver" wobbler, attached to the shelf edge directly below
  Original Roast.
- No other Brightmoor point-of-sale material may be displayed.

## Look and feel

The display should look inviting, premium and well maintained throughout the
campaign. Shoppers should immediately notice the Original Roast offer.

## Commercial terms and reporting

- Larkfield Market receives a promotional fixture fee of £45 per store for the
  campaign period, payable only for stores passing the week-one audit.
- Store teams replenish the Brightmoor block at least twice daily.
- Field reps audit each store in weeks one and three and upload the audit
  photographs required by the execution standards (section 6).
- The regional manager reports weekly sales against target every Monday.
