# Campaign brief: Hollin Roasters "2 for £7"

**Brand:** Hollin Roasters Ltd.
**Retailer:** Larkfield Market, all stores with a hot-beverages aisle
**Campaign period:** 1 June to 30 June 2027
**Display set-up deadline:** 31 May 2027

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.
> This brief belongs to a different brand from the Brightmoor briefs. The same
> bay photographs can be audited against both brands' requirements.

## Objective

Grow basket size with a multibuy on Hollin Classic, our core blend. Hollin
Classic must be easy to find and the multibuy price impossible to miss.

## Range and placement

- Hollin Classic (red pack) is placed on the **top shelf** of the coffee bay.
- Minimum **four facings** of Hollin Classic, side by side.
- Hollin Classic must not be split across shelves.

## Pricing

Larkfield Market has agreed a "2 for £7" multibuy on Hollin Classic for this
campaign, funded by Hollin.

- A yellow promotional shelf-edge label reading **"2 for £7"** directly below
  the Hollin Classic facings.
- The single-pack price must still be shown, as the Price Marking Order 2004
  requires; Larkfield Market may show it on the same label or a separate one.

## Point-of-sale material

- One **"2 for £7" header card** clipped above the Hollin Classic facings,
  readable from the aisle.

## Store responsibilities

- Hollin Classic must stay fully stocked. An empty facing on a Saturday costs
  us more than a week of advertising.
- Store teams must not move Hollin products to make room for other brands'
  promotions.
- Stores receive a £30 display incentive, conditional on compliant audit
  photographs in week two: the full coffee bay, plus a close-up of the top
  shelf showing the multibuy label and header card legibly.
