# Campaign brief: Brightmoor Hazelnut launch

**Brand:** Brightmoor Coffee Co.
**Retailer:** Larkfield Market, all stores with a hot-beverages aisle
**Campaign period:** 29 June to 9 August 2027
**Display set-up deadline:** 29 June 2027, before store opening
**Read with:** *Larkfield Market: Brightmoor Coffee execution standards*, v3.1

> Fictional brands and retailer, created for a Microsoft-Decision-1.1 demonstration.

## Objective

Launch Brightmoor Hazelnut, the first flavoured coffee in the range, and secure
its place next to our best seller. This campaign immediately follows "Summer
Saver"; all Summer Saver material must be gone on day one.

## Range and placement

The Brightmoor range stays on the **eye-level (middle) shelf**. Shelf-edge
prices are those agreed with Larkfield Market for the launch; Brightmoor funds
the introductory price.

| SKU | Pack colour | Minimum facings | Agreed shelf-edge label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original Roast | Blue | 2 | £4.00 white label |
| **Hazelnut (new)** | Orange | 2 | **£3.00** yellow introductory-price label |
| Dark Roast | Black | 2 | £4.00 white label |
| Decaf | Green | 2 | £4.00 white label |

Hazelnut must sit **immediately to the right of Original Roast**, so shoppers
who reach for our best seller see the new flavour next to it. Arrange the range
from left to right: **Original Roast, Hazelnut, Dark Roast, Decaf.**

Original Roast returns to its standard £4.00 shelf price, as agreed with
Larkfield Market. No £3.50 labels may remain.

## Point-of-sale material

- One **"NEW" shelf strip** running along the shelf edge beneath the Hazelnut
  facings.
- The "Summer Saver" wobbler from the previous campaign must be removed.
- No other Brightmoor point-of-sale material may be displayed.

## Launch standards

Because launches are judged in their first fortnight, Hazelnut must never show
an empty facing. Store teams should check the block every two hours during
opening hours and record replenishment on the launch checklist.

## Commercial terms and reporting

- Larkfield Market receives a launch listing fee, invoiced separately.
- Field reps audit each store within three days of launch and again in week
  four, uploading the audit photographs required by the execution standards
  (section 6).
- Head office will review Hazelnut rate of sale after six weeks to confirm the
  permanent listing.
